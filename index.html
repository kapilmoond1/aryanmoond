Welcome to Hindi Alphabet Learning

Hindi is written in Devanagari script and has 52 letters: 13 vowels and 39 consonants. Click on any letter to hear its pronunciation and learn more about it.

स्वर (Vowels)

अ
a

Pronunciation: "a" as in "about"

Example: अनार (anār) - Pomegranate

आ
ā

Pronunciation: "aa" as in "father"

Example: आम (ām) - Mango

इ
i

Pronunciation: "i" as in "sit"

Example: इमली (imlī) - Tamarind

ई
ī

Pronunciation: "ee" as in "feet"

Example: ईख (īkh) - Sugarcane

उ
u

Pronunciation: "u" as in "put"

Example: उल्लू (ullū) - Owl

ऊ
ū

Pronunciation: "oo" as in "boot"

Example: ऊन (ūn) - Wool

ऋ
ṛ

Pronunciation: "ri" as in "rich"

Example: ऋषि (ṛṣi) - Sage

ए
e

Pronunciation: "e" as in "bed"

Example: एक (ek) - One

ऐ
ai

Pronunciation: "ai" as in "aisle"

Example: ऐनक (ainak) - Glasses

ओ
o

Pronunciation: "o" as in "go"

Example: ओस (os) - Dew

औ
au

Pronunciation: "au" as in "caught"

Example: औरत (aurat) - Woman

अं
aṃ

Pronunciation: Nasal sound

Example: अंगूर (aṃgūr) - Grapes

अः
aḥ

Pronunciation: Aspirated sound

Example: दुःख (duḥkh) - Sorrow

व्यंजन (Consonants)

क
ka

Pronunciation: "k" as in "kite"

Example: कमल (kamal) - Lotus

ख
kha

Pronunciation: Aspirated "k"

Example: खरगोश (khargosh) - Rabbit

ग
ga

Pronunciation: "g" as in "go"

Example: गाय (gāy) - Cow

घ
gha

Pronunciation: Aspirated "g"

Example: घर (ghar) - House

ङ
ṅa

Pronunciation: Nasal "ng"

Example: पंख (paṅkh) - Wing

च
ca

Pronunciation: "ch" as in "chair"

Example: चाय (cāy) - Tea

छ
cha

Pronunciation: Aspirated "ch"

Example: छाता (chātā) - Umbrella

ज
ja

Pronunciation: "j" as in "jump"

Example: जल (jal) - Water

झ
jha

Pronunciation: Aspirated "j"

Example: झंडा (jhaṇḍā) - Flag

ञ
ña

Pronunciation: "ny" as in "canyon"

Example: ज्ञान (jñān) - Knowledge
